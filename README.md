## 📖 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI dashboard** developed to analyze online retail sales data and deliver actionable business insights for executive decision-making.

The dashboard was created as part of the **Tata Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights** virtual job simulation offered by **Forage**, where I assumed the role of a **Data Analyst**. The objective was to transform raw retail transaction data into executive-level dashboards that support strategic business decisions.

---

## 🏢 Business Scenario

The executive team required an interactive dashboard to better understand the company's sales performance, customer behavior, and market opportunities.

Using Power BI, I designed visualizations that answer key business questions and enable stakeholders to monitor business performance through an intuitive and data-driven interface.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance
* Identify the countries generating the highest revenue
* Discover top-performing customers
* Evaluate customer purchasing behavior
* Create executive-friendly visualizations
* Provide business recommendations based on data

## ❓ Business Questions

This dashboard answers the following business questions:

* Which countries generate the highest revenue?
* Which customers contribute the highest revenue?
* What is the revenue distribution across countries?
* Which customers should receive additional business attention?
* How can executives use these insights to improve business performance?

## 💡 Key Insights

* Revenue is concentrated among a small number of countries.
* High-value customers contribute a significant portion of overall revenue.
* Customer purchasing patterns reveal opportunities for retention strategies.
* Geographic sales analysis helps identify markets for future expansion.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

* Develop customer loyalty programs for high-value customers.
