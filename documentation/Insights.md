# Business Insights

## Project Context

This dashboard was developed during the **Tata Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights** virtual job simulation offered through **Forage**.

The objective was to create executive-level visualizations that transform retail sales data into actionable business insights.

---

## Key Findings

### Revenue

Revenue analysis identifies the countries generating the highest sales, helping management prioritize future investments.

### Customers

A relatively small number of customers account for a significant share of total revenue, highlighting opportunities for customer retention strategies.

### Products

Top-selling products contribute substantially to overall revenue and should remain a focus for inventory planning.

### Business Trends

Sales trends reveal seasonal fluctuations that can support demand forecasting and promotional planning.

---

# Recommendations

- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing countries.
- Develop loyalty initiatives for high-value customers.
- Maintain inventory for top-selling products.
- Monitor sales trends regularly to improve business forecasting.
