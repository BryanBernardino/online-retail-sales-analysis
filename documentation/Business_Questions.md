# Business Questions

## Project Background

This project was completed as part of the **Tata Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights** virtual job simulation on **Forage**.

In this simulation, I assumed the role of a Data Analyst tasked with creating visualizations and business insights to support executive decision-making for an online retail business.

---

## Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer the following business questions:

### Revenue Analysis

- Which countries generate the highest revenue?
- Which countries should be prioritized for business expansion?

### Customer Analysis

- Which customers contribute the highest revenue?
- Who are the most valuable customers?

### Product Performance

- Which products generate the highest sales?
- Which products should receive additional marketing attention?

### Sales Trends

- How has revenue changed over time?
- Are there noticeable seasonal patterns?

### Executive Decision Support

The dashboard provides interactive visualizations that enable executives to monitor performance, identify trends, and make informed business decisions.
